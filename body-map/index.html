Where does it hurt?

Tap where the patient points. You get that region's own chart first, then the charts that cut across regions (arthritis, infection, nerves, fractures). Switch to Which structure to tap a single ligament, tendon, muscle or bursa and open its chart with that diagnosis already lit. Anatomy tables puts the bones, muscles, ligaments, cranial nerves, brachial plexus and blood supply on one skeleton, each switched on or off from its Show bar: tap a muscle for its action, nerve and clinical notes, a cranial nerve for its table row on the body, any part of the plexus to light its path back to the roots, an artery, ligament or bone for what this year's slides say about it. Everything follows the slides: what they leave out is left out. Dermatome map is the dermatome model from the anatomy tables. Root or nerve? puts a pinched root on one arm and a compressed nerve on the other, so you can see why a root's numb patch is fuzzy and spread across nerves while a nerve's is sharp-edged.
