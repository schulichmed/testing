# testing

Prototypes for the Schulich pre-clerkship portal team to try out and give feedback on before anything reaches the live site ([schulichmed/preclerkship](https://github.com/schulichmed/preclerkship)).

Each prototype is a self-contained folder with an `index.html`. Open `index.html` at the root for the list.

| Prototype | What it asks |
|---|---|
| [`clinical-skills/`](clinical-skills/) | The clinical skills course for Years 1 and 2, in the portal's own structure and styled by the live portal's `base.css` and `portal.css`: a course page of four block cards (history taking, physical exam, ultrasound, OSCE prep); one block page per section of topic cards, each with a Servier Medical Art illustration (CC BY-SA 3.0, credited in `img/credits.json` and at the foot), the courses that teach it (FoM → PoM 1 when it comes back in depth) and what it covers; and `landing.html`, the portal landing with Clinical Skills as a fifth card. Only [`msk-exam/`](msk-exam/) is built; topics without a page are dimmed. `clinical-skills/build.py` writes every page from its `SECTIONS` and `SKILLS` lists |
| [`body-map/`](body-map/) | One 3D body (BodyParts3D skeleton, muscles and skin, CC BY 4.0) with six views: **Where it hurts** (tap a region: its own MSK charts first, then the cross-cutting ones, each opening in a pop-up), **Which structure** (tap a ligament, tendon, muscle or bursa: its chart opens with that diagnosis lit, via `charts/<chart>/#col=<id>`), **Anatomy tables** (one skeleton with three layers switched on and off from its Show bar: the table muscles, tap one for its row and hide it to see underneath; the cranial nerves, right side, a drawn brainstem with each nerve through its opening in the see-through skull to what it supplies, using BodyParts3D's real orbital nerves, tap one and its Origin, Exits via and Fibres and function columns sit on the body with the whole row in the panel; and the brachial plexus, right side, roots from their real foramina, trunks, divisions, cords around the axillary artery and branches beside the arm's arteries, tap a part to light its path back to the roots with the plexus tab's notes; the bones and the ligaments (drawn from bony landmarks), and the blood supply, right side, only the arteries this year's slides name, BodyParts3D's own vessels with the few it lacks drawn from landmarks, tap any of them for what the slides say about it. The whole view follows this year's lecture slides (`body-map/slides.json`, merged by `slides_merge.py` from the OneNote decks): each muscle's action, nerve and clinical points as the slides give them, and anything the slides leave out is left out; the muscles turn see-through when a nerve or artery layer is on) **Dermatome map** (the anatomy tables' Dermatomes tab, framed as it is there), **Myotome map** (the table muscles coloured by the root whose test movement they do, C5-T1 and L2-S1; pick a root to light its test muscles and, paler, the others it supplies, with its test movement, reflex, everyday function, key sensory point and, for cervical roots, the disc that pinches it; tap a muscle for its roots; a which-root quiz from a movement or an everyday function. Movements, reflexes and muscle levels follow this year's slides; the everyday functions are bedside teaching and say so) and **Root or nerve?** (a pinched root on the patient's right arm, its dermatome faded at the edges and the plexus lighting every nerve it feeds, beside a compressed nerve on the left arm, its cutaneous patch hard-edged and only that nerve lit: C8 or ulnar, C6 or median; plus every cutaneous nerve's patch coloured as on Netter N400). `?view=derm` opens the painted dermatomes on their own, which the anatomy tables show as their Dermatome levels tab. `body-map/build_body.py`, `body-map/build_layers.py`, `body-map/build_nerves.py`, `body-map/build_arteries.py` and `body-map/build_ligaments.py` rebuild the models |
| [`charts/`](charts/) | The PoM 2 interactive overview charts the body map opens (Look, Walk, Test); `#col=<id>` opens one with that diagnosis focused, `?embed=1` drops its header for the pop-up. Each MSK diagnosis column carries a picture under its name (a mechanism diagram, imaging or microscopy; tap for full size) from `charts/figures.json` and `charts/img/<chart>/`: openly licensed (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY open-access figures; public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA only), credited at the foot of each chart, with a few drawn for the page where nothing open existed; no patient photos |
| [`fom-pom1/`](fom-pom1/) | 81 overview charts for the clinical lectures of FoM and PoM 1, drawn in the PoM 2 structure (content files in the preclerkship repo's `tools/pathways/src/`) and made interactive (Look, Walk, Test) by `fom-pom1/build.py` |
| [`anatomy-tables/`](anatomy-tables/) | One page of anatomy tabs in lecture order: Andy's cranial nerve and muscle tables (each clinical note on the muscle it is about; Test hides a column until tapped), a drawn cranial nerve map (where each nerve leaves the brain, the opening it leaves the skull by, what it supplies; click a nerve for its table row or an opening for what passes through; quiz on openings, sensory/motor/both, functions and naming), the dermatome map, the painted dermatomes from the body map (Dermatome levels), and an interactive brachial plexus (click a part to light its path; quiz on roots, origins and what each nerve supplies). `anatomy-tables/build.py` rebuilds it from `vault_tables.json`, `plexus.html` and `template.html` |
| [`dermatomes/`](dermatomes/) | Shown as the Dermatomes tab of [`anatomy-tables/`](anatomy-tables/#dermatomes) (framed with `?embed=1`); still opens on its own. The JABSOM dermatome model from Sketchfab, embedded, with the C5 to C8/T1 and L2 to S1 table: click a level to fly to its test point, test yourself by naming or finding the point. Below it, a 3D spine (BodyParts3D bones, CC BY 4.0) with the cord and all 31 pairs of roots at their real exit levels, linked to the skin model; `dermatomes/build_spine.py` rebuilds it |
| [`pelvic-pain/`](pelvic-pain/) | The existing pelvic pain overview chart made interactive: focus a diagnosis, walk its questions, or hide a row to test yourself |
| [`pom2-note-visuals/`](pom2-note-visuals/) | Four ways (A to D) to work mnemonic tiles, numbers to know, tricky points and pearls into three PoM 2 notes, below the existing interactive chart |
| [`chart-merge/`](chart-merge/) | Five ways (A to E) to combine a lecture note's overview chart with the diagnostic flowcharts, on the thyroid example |
