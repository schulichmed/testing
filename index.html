Prototypes

Mockups for the pre-clerkship portal team to give feedback on. Nothing here is on the live site.

  • Clinical skills, as a course: in the portal's own structure, opening straight on History taking; the block bar moves between history taking, physical exam, ultrasound and OSCE prep, each a page of topic cards. Each topic page opens on a bar of every topic in its section, so one tap reaches any other (see MSK, OB/Gyn history); plus the portal landing with Clinical Skills as a fifth card
  • Neurological exam, top down: laid out like the MSK page by the shared exam engine. The general approach, then mental status, cranial nerves, motor, sensory, reflexes, coordination, gait and where the lesion is, each manoeuvre grouped under what it tests, with Test mode, and the 59 neurology videos from Western's Health Education Media Library, each played when tapped, and the faculty's photos of 30 manoeuvres from the same library
  • MSK exam, by region: every exam from the MSK clinical skills suggested-videos list. The general exam first (approach to every joint, gait, the neuro screen table), then for each region its look, feel and move, its special tests grouped under the problem you suspect (each with a picture, numbered steps and what counts as positive), and its pearls; Test mode hides each part until you tap
  • MSK body map, in 3D: tap where the patient points to get that region's charts first, then the ones that cut across regions; tap a single ligament, tendon or bursa to open its chart with that diagnosis lit; tap a muscle for its anatomy table row; or see the dermatomes painted on the skin, labelled, with the key sensory points and a find-the-level quiz; or the myotomes, the muscles coloured by the root that powers them, with each root's test movement, reflex and everyday function and a which-root quiz
  • Visual elements inside the PoM 2 notes: tiles, numbers, tricky points and pearls worked into the note, four versions (A to D)
  • FoM and PoM 1 overview charts, interactive: 81 drawn overview charts for the clinical lectures of Foundations of Medicine and PoM 1, built the same way as the PoM 2 charts, with Look, Walk and Test
  • Anatomy tables: cranial nerves and muscles: Andy's cranial nerve and muscle tables, back to foot in lecture order, a drawn cranial nerve map (Cranial nerve map: brain, opening in the skull, what it supplies), with a Test mode that hides the nerve or action column until tapped, the 3D dermatome map (its own tab, Dermatomes), plus an interactive brachial plexus: click a root, cord or nerve to light its path, or quiz yourself on roots, origins and what each nerve supplies
  • Pelvic pain overview, interactive: the existing overview chart with Look, Walk and Test modes
  • Merging note charts with the flowcharts: five versions (A to E)
