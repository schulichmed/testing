Visual elements inside the PoM 2 notes

Each note already opens with its interactive chart, so nothing new goes at the top. Instead the mnemonic tiles, numbers to know, tricky points and pearls are worked into the note itself. Three real PoM 2 notes, four ways (A to D) of doing it.
