A dermatome is the strip of skin supplied by one spinal nerve root. The ten levels on the table we have to memorise are in colour; every other level is grey (hover or tap it to see which it is). Drag sideways to turn both figures, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Click a level to light it up on both figures. Click it again to see everything.

The map is a teaching simplification: real dermatomes overlap and vary between people, and charts disagree at the edges. This one follows Keegan and Garrett's map, the one Netter's atlas draws; the table is the version we are examined on. The face is supplied by the trigeminal nerve (cranial nerve V), not a spinal root.
