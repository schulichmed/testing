Dermatome map, in 3D

A dermatome is the strip of skin supplied by one spinal nerve root. Turn the model with your finger or mouse, and click a level on the right to fly to its test point. The colour families follow the usual atlas layout: purple and blue for cervical, red to yellow for thoracic, green for lumbar, pink for sacral.

Every colour band on the model is labelled with its level; the ten on our table are in purple. Labels that would overlap are left out, so zoom in to see them all. Click a level to fly to its test point; click it again to go back to the whole body.

Each level is marked at its classic test point (the spot a sensory exam checks), on the model's left side; the right side is the mirror image. Real dermatomes overlap and vary between people. The table is the version we are examined on.
