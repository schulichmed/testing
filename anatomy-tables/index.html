Spaces, landmarks and nerve injuries

Proximal median nerve lesionsHand of benediction when trying to make fist: median nerve compression in cubital fossa or between 2 heads of pronator teres → loss of flexion of proximal interphalangeal joints (PIPs)/distal interphalangeal joints (DIPs)/metacarpophalangeal joints (MPs) of digits 2-3
Distal median nerve lesionCarpal tunnel syndrome (flexor retinaculum over flexor pollicis longus (FPL)/flexor digitorum superficialis (FDS)/flexor digitorum profundus (FDP)/median nerve): median nerve compression in carpal tunnel → paresthesia of lateral 3.5 digits, paralysis/wasting of thenar muscles and lateral 2 lumbricals; test via Tinel's sign of wrist
Proximal ulnar nerve lesionsClaw hand: compression posterior to medial epicondyle or between 2 heads of flexor carpi ulnaris; lower brachial plexus injuries Klumpke paralysis C8-T1 → clawing of digits 4-5 (hyperextended MPs, cannot flex DIPs/extend interphalangeal joints (IPs) of digits 4-5)Waiter's tip arm position: upper brachial plexus injuries Erb-Duchenne palsy C5-6 → adduct shoulder, medially rotate arm, extend elbow; paresthesia lateral upper limb
Distal ulnar nerve lesionUlnar canal syndrome: ulnar nerve compression in Guyon's canal → decreased sensation of medial 1.5 digits, weak intrinsic hand muscles; clawing of digits 4-5 possible but flexion unaffected and no radial deviation
